The theme for 2023 was "(R)EVOLUTION," meant to be read as both "revolution" and "evolution."

As a first step we brainstormed different visual concepts around this idea and these were the initial sketches I presented.

<div class="outdent-wide gallery">
  ![](sketch-1.png) ![](sketch-2.png) ![](sketch-3.png) ![](sketch-4.png)
</div>

## Logos

We liked certain design elements from all of these and decided to combine them for the final logo. Our applications use two different elements: concentric circles that spell out "REVOLUTION" as well as a shortened, smaller version that serves more as an icon for smaller spaces.

<div class="outdent-max">![](logos.png)</div>

## Mockups

From this we built out the rest of the identity. The concentric circles could be used in a variety of ways: a framing device, as a subtle pattern in the background, blown up to serve as shapes, etc.

Given this was a TED-sponsored event, we wanted to keep with their [colors and font choices](https://www.ted.com/tedx/organizer-guide/design-and-promote-event#technical-specifications), namely red/black/white and Helvetica.[^1]

## Applications

To announce the speakers I applied the concentric circles as a framing device for the headshots that each of them provided.
