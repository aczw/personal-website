At Penn I was part of the TEDxPenn Design team for 2 years. Every year, we come up with an overarching theme for the conference, which affects everything from the speakers we choose to the visual identity.

As part of the Design team it was our responsibility to interpret the theme visually and create the overall branding for that year's conference.

Above depicts the identity for TEDxPenn 2023: (R)EVOLUTION, which is the project that I was most involved in during my time at the club.
