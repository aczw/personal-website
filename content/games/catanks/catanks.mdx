During my year as co-president of [UPGRADE](https://pennupgrade.com), Penn's game dev club, we set out to develop a game that involved every willing club member, with the end goal of publishing it on Steam.

I'm happy to say that Catanks is free to play on Steam! Go play it.

I also wrote a little about the development process in [this write-up](/projects/catanks) for the project if you're curious.
