Overhauled the design language, again.

On some level I feel that v8 is what v7 should have been. In v7 I never finished the gallery view (it remained grayed out for its entire existence), and I feel that the dither shader wasn't being utilized in the best way.

Page widths have been vastly simplified, with 3 widths to choose from: prose (550px), wide (900px), and max (1200px). Everything has been left aligned, as God intended, and every page was redesigned.

Gallery has become the Index, which collects every content collection on the site together under one page. This is important because I'm keeping the header to just my projects and writing.

As the first new content collection, I've added games that I've made or helped work on under [/games](/games).
